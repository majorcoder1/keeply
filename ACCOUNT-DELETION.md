# Delete your Keeply account

To delete your Keeply account and all of its data (notes, folders, labels, images, voice recordings, transcripts and summaries), email **aaron@m4j.net** from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Keeply account".

We will delete the account and everything in it within 30 days and confirm by email. Nothing is kept afterwards.

You can also delete individual notes and recordings at any time inside the app.

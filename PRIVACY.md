# Privacy policy — Keeply

Keeply (also called 4thepeople Notes) is published by 4The People Media (Aaron McCreery, Crossville, Tennessee, USA). The app is a window onto the notes service at https://keeply.4thepeople.live.

**What we store.** To keep your notes in sync you create an account. We store your name, your email address, your password (only as a one-way hash), your two-factor sign-in setup, and your notes, folders, labels, photos and voice recordings.

**Your notes are locked on your device.** Notes (titles and text), labels, the names of folders you create, photos, voice recordings and recording titles are encrypted on your own phone or computer (AES-256) with a 24-digit key that is made on your device and never sent to us. We store only the locked versions, so we cannot read them. We can still see when you sign in, when notes were saved, how many you have and how large they are, a note's color and whether it is pinned, which folder it is in (by number), and the names of the three starter folders (Record, Work and Personal).

**No key recovery.** Because we never have your key, we cannot recover it. If you lose it and every device that has it, your notes cannot be opened. You can erase the account's contents and start again.

**Two-factor sign-in.** Signing in needs a code from an authenticator app. We store the shared secret (encrypted) and hashed one-time recovery codes.

**Microphone.** Keeply uses the microphone only when you start a voice note. Recordings are locked on your device before they are uploaded. Keeply does not turn recordings into text or summaries, and no note, photo or recording is sent to any third-party service.

**Link previews (off by default).** If you turn on rich link previews in Settings, our server looks up the web addresses you preview to fetch the page's title and picture. It sees those addresses, but never the note around them, and it keeps nothing.

**Sharing.** The Share button sends a note's words to the app you choose. That happens on your device, by your choice.

**No ads, no selling, no tracking.** We do not sell or share your data and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

**Security.** Data travels over HTTPS. Sign-in attempts are rate limited, and changing or resetting a password signs your other devices out. The Android app is kept out of phone backups, blocks screenshots by default, and refuses plain-HTTP connections.

**Taking your data with you.** In Settings you can export all your notes, photos and recordings as one .zip. The export is made on your device and is not locked.

**Deleting your data.** You can delete notes and recordings inside the app at any time. To delete your whole account and everything in it, see ACCOUNT-DELETION.md or email aaron@m4j.net.

**Children.** Keeply is not directed at children under 13.
